## Current Capstone Research

### From Ribbon to Region: Spatio-Temporal Analysis of Emerging Coalescence along the GT Road Corridor (1995–2025)

This BS research capstone examines urban coalescence along approximately 320 km of the GT Road corridor in Punjab.

- Analyze Landsat and GHSL datasets in Google Earth Engine to map historical built-up expansion.
- Apply Random Forest classification with NDVI, NDBI, and MNDWI spectral indices.
- Develop 10 km × 10 km cross-section continuity profiles to measure inter-urban gaps and gap-closure timelines.
- Combine landscape metrics, DMSP/VIIRS night-time lights, and a 200-household KoboToolbox survey to examine hidden urbanization and socioeconomic transformation.
- Conduct the research under the supervision of **Humayun Ashraf**, Lecturer, Department of Geography and GIS, University of Gujrat.
